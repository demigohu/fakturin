## How to use it

Open **Check** and type a Faktur Pajak number. Any format works: dots, dashes, spaces and replacement suffixes are ignored, so `040.026-00012.34567` and `04002600012345` find the same invoice.

The page computes the invoice ID locally, `keccak256("FP:" + digits)`, and reads it from `FakturRegistry`. Nothing is sent to a Fakturin server.

## Reading the result

| Status | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| Never submitted | Never financed on Fakturin. |
| Checking | Chainlink is checking it with the tax office right now. |
| Rejected | Not verified, with the reason. May be submitted again. |
| Raising funds | Verified, raising funds now. Not financed yet. |
| Financed | Already financed. Nobody else can finance this Faktur Pajak. |
| Repaid | Financed once and fully repaid. It can never be financed again. |
| Defaulted | Financed once and not repaid. It can never be financed again. |
| Funding failed | Funding did not fill in time and lenders were refunded. May be submitted again. |

Under the verdict you see:

- **Funding progress** while it is raising funds.
- The **invoice NFT**, drawn onchain, with its current stamp. After the invoice closes the NFT is burned, but the picture stays with the final LUNAS or DEFAULT stamp.
- **Facts from the chain**: amount, due date and whether the seller wallet has defaulted before.
- **Tax-office data** (simulated PJAP): seller and buyer names and the masked NPWP.
- **Onchain details**: invoice ID, seller wallet, vault address.

<Callout title="What is onchain">
  The registry stores only the invoice ID, hashes of the NPWPs, the amount and the dates. Business names come from the tax office API, not from the chain.
</Callout>
