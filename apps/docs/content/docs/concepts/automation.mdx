---
title: Automatic closing (keeper)
description: Why nobody presses "default" or "cancel" in Fakturin, and how the FakturKeeper contract and a Chainlink CRE cron close overdue vaults.
---

Two things must happen on time without anyone wanting to do them: cancel a vault whose funding window ended unfilled, and default a vault whose seller did not repay. Lenders should not have to press "Mark default" against a business. `FakturKeeper` plus a cron workflow do it.

## The contract

| Function | Who | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `actionFor(vault)` | view | `CancelFunding`, `MarkDefault` or `None`, from the vault's state and deadlines |
| `closable(max)` | view | Up to `max` vaults that can be closed now (scans the factory's vault list) |
| `onReport(meta, report)` | Chainlink forwarder only | Closes the vaults in `abi.decode(report, (address[]))` |
| `closeDue(max)` | anyone | Permissionless fallback: closes up to `max` due vaults |

Each close runs in `try/catch` with a fixed gas budget (`CLOSE_GAS = 250 000`), so one vault that was already closed by somebody else never blocks the rest. If the remaining gas cannot cover another close, the loop stops; if it cannot cover even one, the call reverts with `InsufficientGas` instead of silently doing nothing.

## Modes

| Mode | Who closes | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `cre` | `keeper` workflow via `scripts/keeper-loop.sh` (pm2) | Every `KEEPER_INTERVAL` seconds (default 60) |
| `relayer` | The app's `/api/keeper`, calling `closeDue` | When a screen shows a vault past its deadline |
| Anyone | `closeDue`, `cancelFunding`, `markDefault` directly | Any time after the deadline |

The deadlines are checked onchain in every path, so the keeper cannot close anything early, and needs no special role.
